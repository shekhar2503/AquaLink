Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE)[1][2] is an autonomous engineering college in the city of Pune, India, established in 1999.[3]

The Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering is affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), formerly the University of Pune.[4] The university houses 46 academic departments. It has about 307 recognised research institutes and 612 affiliated colleges offering graduate and undergraduate courses.

Technical Events

[edit]

To provide a platform for budding engineers to showcase their technical abilities and knowledge PCCOE organizes intercollegiate events "TECHLLIGENT" and "SPECTRUM". SPECTRUM is a unique event in SPPU for First-Year Engineering Students. TECHLLIGENT consists of events and competitions related to models, projects, papers, demonstrations, and coding. Students of PCCOE have active participation in international level competitions like "ROBOCON", 'SAE BAJA', 'Supra', 'ESVC3000',[5] ACM ICPC, and Smart India Hackathon (SIH). An event called "ZEST" is organized by the Department of MBA.
